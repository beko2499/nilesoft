# NILESOFT — 2D Hero Illustration Prompts

استخدم **كتلة الأسلوب الثابتة** مع كل واحد من البرومبتات التسعة أدناه.

## كتلة الأسلوب الثابتة

```text
Create a premium flat 2D editorial vector illustration for NILESOFT's new visual identity.

Visual language: geometric minimalism, bold clear silhouettes, modular square shapes, diagonal cuts inspired by the NILESOFT mark, precise grid-based construction, strong visual hierarchy, and a clever instantly understandable metaphor.

Color palette only: warm paper #FAFAF8, deep ink #141413, near-black #0C0C0B, soft ivory #F5F5F1, stone gray #8A8A84, mist #DEDED8, and one controlled copper accent #C06B3E.

Composition: universal and aspect-ratio independent, balanced around the center, with generous breathing room and safe cropping on every side. The illustration must remain clear in portrait, square, or landscape crops. Use a small number of large meaningful elements instead of many tiny details.

Style: sophisticated Swiss editorial design blended with modern Arabic Gulf technology branding, clean 2D vector shapes, restrained line work, subtle paper texture only if needed.

Constraints: communicate through a real-world example or visual metaphor, not through generic software dashboards. No text, no letters, no numbers, no generated logo, no watermark. No photorealism, no 3D rendering, no gradients, no glass effects, no glossy materials, no floating dashboard clutter, and no multiple laptops or computer screens. Use at most one simple device only when the concept genuinely requires it.
```

---

## 1. حضور رقمي متكامل

```text
Scene concept: A small ordinary storefront is being transformed into a confident modern landmark using a few large geometric building blocks. The left side begins modest, unfinished, and nearly invisible; the same structure becomes clear, polished, welcoming, and distinctive as the blocks lock into place. A copper diagonal path guides several simple customer figures toward the completed business, while competing storefronts fade quietly into the stone-gray background.

Core message: a complete digital presence helps a business grow and stand out without needing extravagant resources.

Avoid: websites shown on screens, laptops, floating analytics cards, shopping carts, money symbols, or a literal before-and-after split line.
```

## 2. تطبيق في جيب كل عميل

```text
Scene concept: One simplified customer figure is walking naturally with a single phone partly visible from a pocket. The phone acts like a compact personal hub: three large geometric symbols stay closely connected to it—a copper notification pulse, an ivory order parcel, and a circular loyalty token. The customer continues moving while these services remain connected, showing that the brand travels with them everywhere.

Core message: the customer's phone is always close, so the business should be present there through its own app.

Avoid: multiple phones, app-store logos, detailed user interfaces, rows of app icons, floating screen cards, or people standing around a giant phone.
```

## 3. الأتمتة توفر الوقت والمال

```text
Scene concept: A tired worker stands beside a growing pile of loose papers on one side. The papers enter a simple geometric sorting machine built from square modules and one strong diagonal copper element. On the other side, the same work exits automatically as three perfectly organised packages while a large clock hand moves from chaos toward saved time. The worker's posture changes from overwhelmed to relieved.

Core message: repetitive manual work can be turned into an automatic process that saves hours and money.

Avoid: laptops, robots, humanoid AI, gears everywhere, futuristic factories, floating dashboards, or complex machinery.
```

## 4. من فوضى الرسائل إلى نظام

```text
Scene concept: Many irregular message-shaped pieces arrive from different directions like scattered orders. They pass through one bold geometric funnel or sorting gate, then exit as four aligned trays containing neatly organised parcels, invoices, and customer tokens. A single copper square moves through the system to show the path from disorder to control.

Core message: scattered messages and random orders become one organised business system.

Avoid: WhatsApp branding, phones, chat screenshots, laptops, unreadable interface panels, conveyor-belt realism, or excessive icons.
```

## 5. من الفكرة إلى MVP حقيقي

```text
Scene concept: A rough hand-drawn idea begins as a loose paper sketch with imperfect construction lines. The sketch crosses a short geometric bridge made from modular blocks. At the far end, it becomes one complete polished product object ready to launch, with a copper progress line reaching completion and a few small construction pieces settling into their final positions.

Core message: an early idea can become a real launch-ready product through a clear practical build process.

Avoid: literal rockets, multiple phones, computer screens, coding text, light bulbs, magic effects, or an overly technical blueprint.
```

## 6. الانطباع الأول لا يتكرر

```text
Scene concept: A visitor reaches two neighbouring entrances. One entrance is generic, uncertain, visually weak, and easy to ignore. The other is built from confident ivory and ink geometry, framed by a memorable copper diagonal element, and immediately attracts the visitor's attention. Show the visitor choosing the stronger entrance within a single decisive moment.

Core message: strong identity and presentation win the first impression before any conversation begins.

Avoid: laptop mockups, branding moodboards, business cards, mirrors, beauty imagery, giant logos, or text on storefront signs.
```

## 7. الحضور الرقمي — سباق السيارتين

```text
Scene concept: A dynamic side-view race between exactly two cars. A sleek modern ivory sports car with one controlled copper accent is clearly accelerating ahead on a clean geometric track. Behind it, an older boxy charcoal classic car struggles to keep pace. Strong diagonal speed lines, simple checkpoint blocks, and a clear difference in momentum communicate progress versus falling behind.

Core message: a business with a modern digital presence moves ahead while an outdated competitor falls behind.

Avoid: realistic car brands, logos, city streets, racing crowds, checkered flags, flames, photorealism, 3D cars, dashboards, or more than two vehicles.
```

## 8. استثمار يبني قيمة لسنوات

```text
Scene concept: A business owner places one copper geometric seed into the ground only once. From it grows a structured modular tree whose branches produce repeating symbols of saved time, organised work, and steady growth across several seasons. The tree remains strong while a large circular clock and changing seasonal shapes move quietly around it.

Core message: build the right digital system once and it continues saving time and creating value for years.

Avoid: coins, banknotes, investment charts, stock arrows, laptops, financial dashboards, watering cans, or a childish cartoon tree.
```

## 9. الثقة تُبنى قبل الاتصال

```text
Scene concept: A potential customer stands at the beginning of two possible bridges. One bridge is fragmented, inconsistent, and uncertain. The other is a solid refined geometric bridge built from aligned ivory and ink blocks, secured by a copper keystone and a simple shield-shaped structural element. The customer confidently chooses the stable bridge before meeting anyone on the other side.

Core message: people decide whether a business feels trustworthy before they call or speak to the team.

Avoid: handshakes, review screens, laptops, star-rating cards, padlocks, people at desks, literal phone calls, or generic cybersecurity imagery.
```

## قواعد مراجعة المجموعة

- كل مشهد يجب أن يُفهم خلال ثانيتين دون قراءة النص.
- لا تتكرر نفس الاستعارة أو نفس نوع العنصر الرئيسي بين شريحتين.
- الشاشات ليست اللغة الأساسية؛ الهاتف الوحيد الضروري هو شريحة التطبيق.
- النحاس عنصر توجيه بصري صغير، وليس لونًا يملأ المشهد كله.
- يجب أن تبدو الصور التسع كفصول من نظام بصري واحد عند عرضها متجاورة.
